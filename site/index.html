How OpenBand grows

Your idea can be in the next release.

Most music software is decided behind closed doors. OpenBand is built where everyone can see it: every idea is public, every change is tested, every feature ships as its own release.

You suggest it

Describe what you'd like the studio to do, in your own words. It becomes a public issue on GitHub.

Everyone sees it

Other musicians vote with a 👍 and add their case. The most wanted ideas rise on the roadmap.

It gets built

In the open, with tests that listen to the audio — by contributors and AI coding agents working side by side.

You play it

Each feature is its own release. The app tells you when it's out; the online studio updates by itself.

What it does

A complete studio, made simple.

From the first idea to a master ready for Spotify: instruments, drums, audio, effects, mixing and mastering — each step a gesture, not a manual.

New

Mix & master in one gesture

Pick a genre and a platform: the Mix Pilot balances, EQs, pans, sidechains and masters your song to Spotify, Apple Music or club loudness — every change explained, dosed with one knob.

Create a part in words

“Funk drums”, “arpeggiated piano”, “a lo-fi keys loop”: OpenBand's engines — or the music models you install — play it, in time and in key with your song.

Jam with the AI

Play a phrase and it answers on the next bar, as notes you can keep.

Record like a studio

Microphone or interface, punch-in, takes and comping, blind A/B tests, hum a melody to MIDI.

Never lose an idea

Versions and branches like git for songs, a 30-day time machine, crash recovery.

Play together

Share a link and edit the same song live, with comments — and the AI as the third musician.

Desktop app

Your plugins

Open-source web plugins everywhere, and the CLAP, VST3 and Audio Unit synths and effects installed on your computer, with their own windows and sample-accurate automation — each in its own process, so a crash never stops the music.

Sounds like a record

A true-peak limiter, sidechain, loudness meters and exports for every platform, with stems that add up to the master.

Any AI

Your AI can play it too.

OpenBand speaks MCP, the open standard AI apps use to work with tools. Ask for a bassline, a mix, an arrangement — and watch it happen in the studio, one undo away.

  • In the studio's own chat — Claude, OpenAI, Gemini with your key, or a free model on your computer with Ollama.
  • From your AI app — Claude Desktop, Claude Code, ChatGPT, Codex, Gemini CLI, Cursor, VS Code.
  • It listens before it touches — loudness, balance, masking, harmony: it explains its choices on the notes.
